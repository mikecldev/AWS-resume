An intelligent chatbot on the resume website that answers visitor questions about your experience, skills, and projects using Amazon's AI services. Added a chat widget to the resume website where visitors can ask questions like "What AWS services do you know?" or "Tell me about your projects" and get instant, intelligent responses. The chatbot uses Amazon Lex (same technology powering Alexa) to understand natural language and provide relevant answers. It remembers the conversation context and suggests follow-up questions.
